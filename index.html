Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa to visit Türkiye?

US citizens can obtain a visa on arrival or apply online via e-Visa (evisa.gov.tr) before departure. The process is quick and straightforward.

Is Türkiye safe to visit?

Türkiye is a NATO member and one of the most visited countries in the world. Istanbul, Cappadocia, and Antalya are well-established tourist destinations with strong infrastructure and security.

Is airfare included in the package?

Yes, round-trip airfare from JFK is included in the package.

Is hotel accommodation included?

Yes, accommodation is included for all 11 nights at carefully selected hotels.

Can I bring my family?

Absolutely. This tour welcomes law enforcement officers and their families.

How large is the group?

Group size is limited to 24 participants to ensure a personalized experience.

How much does the tour cost?
